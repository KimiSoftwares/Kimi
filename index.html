Announcement

Welcome to Kimi.

This platform was built from scratch over hours of collaborative coding to provide a seamless, secure, and completely ad-free streaming experience for both YouTube and Spotify without unnecessary clutter.

The platform remains free, stable, and equipped with essential features including history logs, cross-platform link redirection, and customizable particle effects.

Please check back frequently for new software releases and version patches. You can close this announcement window using the button below and check the dashboard section at any time for complete changelogs.
